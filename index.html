About this app

Myth or Fact Quiz Game — a two-player quiz for one big screen and two iPads. The bank holds 40 statements; each round picks 10 at random. The statement and a quick countdown show on the TV screen and on both player tabs; each player taps MYTH or FACT before the countdown ends. A correct answer is worth 10 points — but if both players are correct, only the fastest tap scores and the other gets 0. A wrong answer, or waiting for the countdown to end, scores 0. The correct answer is revealed on the TV screen when the countdown ends or as soon as both players have answered, scores update live on the TV, and after 10 statements the final totals decide the winner.

How a live game runs: in Live, press “Create Today's Room for iPads”, then give the players today's room code — it is shown at the top of Admin and only ever appears in Admin. There is one code for the full day: players open their Player link and enter it once, and their iPad remembers it until midnight, so reopening the room or starting new rounds never asks for it again. Names are per session (activation model): at the start of every session the name bar shows again on each iPad — players enter or change their names fresh, and Admin's New Session button clears the previous session's names and scores so nothing carries over. Their taps count live on the Host screen. Start, reveal, end and reset are all controlled from Live. The Live monitor at the top of Live shows each player's screen link, Connected / Disconnected status, score, correct count, streak, current answer, answer time, recent answers, plus the current question and the timer.

Designing: in Screens you edit a draft of each screen — the canvas previews every change as you make it, but the Host and Player screens keep showing the last published design until you tap Go Live at the bottom of Admin. Go Live means one thing only: it publishes the design live to all screens at once — no file, no download — and saves it in this browser, so it is still here after you reload Admin. Screens on other devices receive it through the live room.
